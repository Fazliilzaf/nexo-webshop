# Design submissions — NEXO Liquid Ritual (ne8xo.com)

## Concept description (EN, for Awwwards / CSSDA / FWA)

**NEXO — Liquid Ritual**

NEXO is a Swedish haircare ritual built for the weeks after a hair transplant. The storefront treats the purchase journey as the ritual itself: visitors don't browse three products, they perform three steps.

The experience opens on a single, viewport-filling scene. No navbar-first layout, no product grid. +1 Lather Me Up fills the screen — matte black packaging, NEXO griege, and a translucent optical layer carrying the commerce. As the visitor drags or scrolls, the material world changes: lather gives way to mist, mist to whip. By the time the bundle appears, the user has physically performed cleanse, condition, protect.

The interface above the scenes borrows the behaviour of optical glass, not the look of glassmorphism. Transparency, refraction and depth are reserved for elements that genuinely float above the content: navigation, ritual progress, the cart. Everything else stays raw and flat.

Typography carries the contrast. Buda Light for product names at monumental scale, a neutral grotesque for commerce, and microscopic mono for the technical layer: INCI names, batch data, the +1/+2/+3 system itself.

The ingredient library continues the idea. Forty-four INCI names float on a dark canvas behind a vertical lens. As each name passes through the lens it sharpens and its plain-Swedish explanation appears. Transparency, made literal.

Even the cart is part of the system: add +1 and it answers "1 of 3. Continue the ritual." Complete the set and it closes with "The ritual is complete."

NEXO was developed together with Hair TP Clinic for people recovering from hair transplants. The site is built to feel like what it sells: calm, precise, and a little obsessive about what goes on your skin.

## Key features to highlight

- Scroll-driven ritual: three full-viewport material worlds (lather, mist, whip) as one continuous sequence
- Optical-layer UI: refraction and depth only where elements physically float above content
- INCI lens: ingredient transparency as an interactive mechanic
- Ritual-aware cart: progress logic (1 of 3 → ritual complete) instead of a generic drawer
- Typography system: Buda Light display × grotesque commerce × mono technical layer
- Full Shopify storefront: live, transactional, custom theme
